About

Bringing traditional Jalisco-style street food culture to South Austin, Tacos Las Amazonas Estilo Jalisco #3 has become a neighborhood staple on South Congress. Built around fresh ingredients, handmade tortillas, and generous portions, the truck serves everyone from early-morning commuters to late-night regulars under a covered outdoor patio with free on-site parking.

With over 1,700 Google reviews, locals keep coming back for the fast turnaround, casual pricing, and the famous salsa bar — whether it's 7:00 AM breakfast tacos or 3:00 AM weekend cravings.
