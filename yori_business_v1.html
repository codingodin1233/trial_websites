Welcome to Yori

More than a meal. A reason to get together.

Some meals are quick stops in a busy day. Others become the part of the day everyone remembers. Korean BBQ has a way of turning dinner into an experience: the grill brings people together, everyone gets involved, and the table becomes the center of the conversation.

That's the spirit behind a visit to Yori Korean BBQ. Whether you're catching up with friends, spending time with family, or simply in the mood for Korean food, there's something special about sitting down, sharing dishes, and enjoying the moment together.

Come hungry, bring good company, and let the table do the rest. We look forward to welcoming you in Malabon.

The Korean BBQ experience

It's not just what's on the plate. It's what happens around it.

Korean BBQ is a different kind of dining experience. Instead of waiting for one finished dish to arrive, the meal unfolds around the table. Food is shared, conversations stretch a little longer, and everyone has a part in the experience.

It's one reason Korean BBQ works so naturally for group meals, celebrations, and casual catch-ups.
