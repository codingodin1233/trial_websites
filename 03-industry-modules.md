## A. Auto repair shop

The visitor is usually stressed: something is broken, they need to know "can you fix it, will you rip me off, when can I come in."

Research:
- Services actually offered (brakes, oil change, diagnostics, A/C, transmission, alignment, suspension, check-engine light, inspections). Use the shop's own wording.
- Vehicles serviced: domestic, Asian, European, diesel, trucks, fleets. `UNKNOWN` if not stated.
- Walk-ins or appointments only? Same-day service? Free estimates?
- Warranty on parts or labor (exact terms, only if published)
- Payment: cards, financing, cash only
- Extras customers mention: towing, shuttle, loaner, after-hours drop-off, Spanish spoken
- Certifications (ASE etc.) ONLY with proof. Otherwise list as `UNVERIFIED CLAIM`
- Review themes about honesty, price, speed, explaining the problem
- Fleet, insurance, or commercial accounts

Site must answer fast: what you fix, where, hours, how to call, whether to come in or book.

## B. Auto body / collision shop

- Services: collision repair, paint, dents, frame, glass, detailing
- Insurance: which insurers are accepted or preferred (only if published); help with claims
- Free estimates, estimate-by-photo, rental car help, towing
- Warranty on paint or repair (exact terms)
- Before/after photos the shop owns
- Review themes: match quality, time to finish, communication

## C. Tire / brake / muffler / glass shop

- Brands carried, new vs used, mobile service
- Prices if published (flat repair, mounting, balancing, alignment)
- Walk-in speed, "while you wait", same-day
- Hours on weekends

## D. Restaurant, BBQ, soul food, wings, bar and grill

- Full menu with prices, with "today's special" or rotating items marked as such
- Dine-in, takeout, delivery (which apps), catering, large orders
- Signature item customers name in reviews
- Hours per day, kitchen close vs bar close
- Parking, patio, groups, reservations, payment
- Alcohol, age restrictions, or live events, only if published

## E. Contractor, logging, construction, trades

- Services and what they do NOT do
- Service area (towns or radius) in the business's own words
- License and insurance numbers or statements, with proof. Never invent
- Typical project types and sizes, only from their own pages or reviews
- Photos of finished work they own
- How to get a quote (call, text, form) and response time customers mention
- Emergency or seasonal availability

## F. Grocery, ice cream, dessert, coffee, hot dog

- Product categories and signature items
- Hours, specials, seasonal items
- Ordering: walk-in, pre-order, catering, cakes by order
- Payment, EBT, local delivery

## Questions every module ends with

1. What is the single most common reason a customer calls or walks in?
2. What do reviewers say they were afraid of before coming?
3. What is the business NOT willing to do (so the site does not promise it)?
